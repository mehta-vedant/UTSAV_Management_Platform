The Data Atlas.

A database table is like a carefully checked spreadsheet. A row is one saved thing. A relationship is a stored link between rows. This page tells you what every UTSAV table owns and how it connects.

Primary key: a row’s permanent ID. Foreign key: a column containing another table’s ID, like an address. Index: a book index that makes common lookups fast. One-to-many: one organization has many expenses; each expense belongs to one organization.

TableWhat one row meansLinks and rules

Questions interviewers ask

Why is OrganizationMember needed? Why not put role on User?

A person can be Treasurer in one organization and Volunteer in another. The role belongs to the membership relationship, not to the person globally.

Why does ExpenseReceipt have both expenseId and organizationId?

The expense proves the parent link; the organization id makes every receipt query tenant-scoped without relying on an accidental join. It is backed by service checks and database foreign keys.

Is this a double-entry ledger?

No. It is approval/audit tracking for donations and expenses. A future double-entry ledger would add immutable journal entries and balanced debit/credit lines.
