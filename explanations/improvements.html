How to improve without buzzwords

Know what exists, then name the next real risk.

A strong engineer does not say “I would add AI and microservices.” They explain a current limit, its impact, the smallest useful fix, and its trade-off.

Interview framework: “Today UTSAV does X. The remaining limitation is Y. I would add Z because it protects or improves A. I would defer it until B because the trade-off is C.”
Good answer

“Today expenses have private receipt evidence and approval history. My next improvement is idempotency because a flaky connection or double click could otherwise create two financial requests. I would use a unique key per user, organization, operation, and request. I would not build microservices first because they do not solve that correctness problem.”
