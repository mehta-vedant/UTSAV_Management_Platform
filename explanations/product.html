Product and problem

UTSAV gives community organizations one accountable workspace.

Instead of scattered WhatsApp messages, paper ledgers and unclear ownership, a committee can manage people, plans and finance with organization-specific permissions.

Operators

Admins, treasurers, committee members and volunteers need role-appropriate work.

Community

Donors and attendees need selected public festival activity and transparency.

Governance

Organizations need records, approvals, auditability and controlled membership.

Interview answer

“UTSAV addresses the governance gap in community events: money, people and commitments exist, but accountability is fragmented. It gives each organization a controlled workspace, while festival organizations can publish selected information. I also know the current event-child tenant-scope gap and would fix it before expanding that workflow.”
