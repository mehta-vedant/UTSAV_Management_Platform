Profile Shortlisting — the real first filter

CGPA cutoff ~8.0–8.5 (but 7.6 has cleared) + resume screening · Kills ~92% of applicants

Most campus drives have no online assessment. Your application/poll form, CGPA, positions of responsibility, projects, and extracurriculars do the filtering. Fill every field carefully.

1

Interview 1 — Technical

~25–35 min · Online · 1 panelist

Resume deep-dive on projects & internships + CS fundamentals (OOPs, DBMS, OS, SQL). Expect every CV line to be dissected.

2

Interview 2 — Technical

~30 min · DSA heavy

"Mainly DSA + CS fundamentals." Linked lists, arrays, strings, search/sort. Strong candidates can be fast-tracked/skipped straight to round 3 (Ishaan was).

3

Interview 3 — Technical + HR (conducted by the CIO)

~30 min · STAR method

Behavioral questions answered with STAR + sorting algorithms, arrays, linked lists + "code a basic search algorithm" on the spot.

4

Interview 4 — HR (conducted by a Co-founder)

~10–15 min · Pure culture fit

Personality, motivation, fit. Interviewers "pay very much attention to you as a person — avoid any negative words."

💻 CS Fundamentals

OOPs
  • Pillars of OOP (inheritance, polymorphism, encapsulation, abstraction)
  • Virtual functions (interview-favorite)
  • Abstraction vs encapsulation
OS / Concurrency
  • Process vs thread
  • Create threads in Java
  • Synchronise two threads on one class
  • Debug C++ multithreading/parallelism code
  • Deadlock, paging / virtual memory
DBMS / SQL
  • Primary vs foreign key
  • SQL basics — DDL/DML/DCL
  • SQL vs NoSQL
  • Indexing
  • SQL query writing (asked in every online panel)
Networks / SE / Other
  • OSI layers, TCP vs UDP
  • Software engineering concepts
  • C basics & pointers/STL (C++)
  • HTML/CSS basics
  • Write linear regression from scratch (robotics track)

👤 Projects & Resume Deep-Dive

  • Defend every line of your CV — projects/internships are the core of offline rounds.
  • Projects in C++ or parallelisation are explicitly rewarded.
  • "Tell me about a challenging bug / technical problem you solved."
  • Be ready to walk through code on your GitHub.
  • Hackathon projects get grilled (Flipkart GRID, Myntra Hackathon came up).

🤝 HR / Behavioral

  • Tell me about yourself
  • Why Addverb? Why this role? — asked in every round
  • STAR-format stories: challenge solved, team under pressure, project owned end-to-end (CIO round)
  • Strengths & weaknesses; salary expectations
  • Willingness to relocate (Noida); 3–5 year goals
  • Availability / joining timeline (internship round)
  • "Do you have any questions for us?" — have one ready, even in tech rounds.
  • Stay 100% positive — negative talk is explicitly penalised.

7. Tips & Red Flags

✅ Do

  • Fill the application/poll form meticulously — it is the first filter.
  • Master DSA (arrays, graphs, DP, strings, linked lists) + C++ (pointers, STL, threads/parallelism).
  • Polish every resume line to interview depth; you will be grilled on it.
  • Practice live SQL queries and Java thread creation/synchronisation.
  • Prepare STAR stories for the CIO/co-founder rounds.
  • Research the company: Zippy sorter, Dynamo AMR, cobots, humanoids, Reliance deal.
  • Have a "questions for us?" prepped. Show approach + confidence even with partial answers.
  • Prepare the relocation answer (Noida).

⚠️ Beware

  • Rejections concentrate at the final co-founder/CEO round (culture-fit / tie-breaker), even after clearing all technicals.
  • Inequality of panel depth between offline and online candidates is reported.
  • Glassdoor: difficulty 3.09/5, positive experience ~54.6% — a real but not brutal bar.
  • Work-life balance criticized (Blind 1.7/5; AmbitionBox Pune 1.7/5); 2023 layoff claims (~150).
  • Promotions/appraisals widely complained about; some report HR pressure during notice periods.
  • Reliance-controlled → decision timelines and headcount can move slowly.
  • Offers may be Intern + FTE (PPO) or Intern-only — check before committing.
